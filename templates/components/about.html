ABOUT FACTSHIELD

Fighting Misinformation Through Artificial Intelligence

FactShield is an AI-powered fake news detection and credibility analysis platform developed to help people evaluate online information before believing or sharing it.

By combining Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, metadata extraction, and Explainable AI, FactShield provides transparent credibility assessments instead of simple true-or-false predictions.

Machine Learning
Credibility Analysis
Publisher Detection
Publication Date Extraction

Why FactShield?

Millions of people consume online news every day, but verifying information remains challenging. FactShield assists users by combining AI with transparent explanations to support informed decision-making.
